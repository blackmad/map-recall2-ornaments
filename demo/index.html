Amsterdam house ornaments

Four-colour Christmas ornaments of Amsterdam buildings and a houseboat, each with a hanging ring sunk into the roof (the houseboat hangs from two eyelets). The houseboat also comes as a 23 cm model without a ring. Each variant is one printable body; the 3MF opens in Snapmaker Orca with filaments 1–4 already assigned. Drag to turn the model.
